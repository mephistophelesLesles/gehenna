    Gehenna’s Gate · Dice System

    Dice System

    The core roll

    d20 + modifier

    Whenever the outcome is uncertain, the headmaster asks for a roll. You roll one twenty-sided die (d20), add the modifier of the stat being tested, and compare the total to a target number the headmaster sets. Meet it or beat it and you succeed; fall short and you fail.

    Example: the headmaster sets the target at 12 and asks for a WIS save. You have WIS 8, so you roll d20 + 8. A 5 on the die makes 13, which succeeds. A 3 makes 11, which fails.

    Stats

    Six stats, six saves

    Your stat’s score is also its modifier, so a stat of 8 means +8 on that roll. Stats start from a fixed base and you add 20 points on top, at most 7 into any one stat.

    • STR raw physical power
    • DEX reflexes and coordination
    • CON endurance and resilience
    • INT reasoning and knowledge
    • WIS awareness and willpower
    • CHA force of personality

    Luck, for or against you

    Advantage and disadvantage

    With advantage you roll two d20 and keep the higher one. With disadvantage you roll two and keep the lower one. Only the die you keep counts, including for a natural 20 or natural 1. The headmaster decides when either applies, and if you have both at once they cancel out.

    Skills

    Damage, healing and shields

    Skills roll their own dice, written like 2d6+3: roll two six-sided dice, add them up, then add 3. A skill can deal damage, heal, or give a shield. Your basic skill can be used freely. Other skills have a cooldown or limit, and your ultimate has a heavy one. You start with 20 HP; at 0, you fall.

    Fighting

    Armor, initiative and turns

    • Initiative: when a fight starts, everyone rolls d20 + their initiative bonus (usually the DEX modifier). Highest goes first, then down the order.
    • Speed: how many feet you can move on your turn.
    • Your turn: move up to your speed and use one skill.
    • Armor: to hit someone, roll d20 + your modifier. If the total meets or beats their Armor, it hits.
    • Damage lowers HP. At 0 HP, you fall.

    Natural 20

    The die shows 20

    A natural number is what the die itself shows, before any modifier.

    • Always a success, whatever the target number or Armor was.
    • Critical hit: on a skill that deals damage, heals or shields, roll its dice twice and add them together. The flat bonus is added only once.
    • The headmaster may add something extra for a truly spectacular result.

    Natural 1

    The die shows 1

    Same idea in reverse: the number on the die, before any modifier.

    • Always a failure, however high your modifier or the target’s Armor is.
    • Fumble: the headmaster may add a complication, such as the skill going wrong, a lost turn, or an unwanted attention.
    • A skill used on a natural 1 still goes on cooldown.

    Unsure about a ruling? The headmaster has the final word.

    Soul's Core

    Banned abilities

    1. Continuous or follow-up attacks (attacks that do 2 or more hits in one turn).
    2. Luck-related abilities. This roleplay relies heavily on dice, so this saves everyone the hassle.
    3. Abilities that are obviously banned, such as one-shot, nullify, steal or copy. If you feel uncertain, ask the headmaster.
