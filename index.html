The setting

Everyone arrives the same way.

Gehenna has no heaven and no hell. It has a trial, and every soul has to stand in it.

The judge

The headmaster

The headmaster calculates your accumulated karma and decides whether you are a sinner or a savior. A punishment or a reward is given to the souls who lived their life.

The treasure

Your core item

Once you play through the prologue, your most treasured item is given to you. It carries the abilities your soul needs to survive.

The rules of chance

Dice decide

This roleplay relies heavily on dice. Your six stats are used for saving throw checks.

The world

Human souls only

Your character must be human, from any year you like. Magic doesn't exist in this world, so your lore has to fit that.

Soul's Core

Banned abilities

  1. Continuous or follow-up attacks (attacks that do 2 or more hits in one turn).
  2. Luck-related abilities. This roleplay relies heavily on dice, so this saves everyone the hassle.
  3. Abilities that are obviously banned, such as one-shot, nullify, steal or copy. If you feel uncertain, ask the headmaster.
