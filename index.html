    Guideline

    Share your character's lore before they died and how they died. Your lore can be anything, but it must fit this world. For example, you can be a king from the past, but there is no magic, since magic doesn't exist in this world.

    Your character must be human, and the year can be any time you like.

    Guideline

    Soul's Core

    Banned abilities

    1. Continuous or follow-up attacks (attacks that do 2 or more hits in one turn).
    2. Luck-related abilities. This roleplay relies heavily on dice, so this saves everyone the hassle.
    3. Abilities that are obviously banned, such as one-shot, nullify, steal or copy. If you feel uncertain, ask the headmaster.
    Core Most Treasured Item

    Guideline

    The item that will eventually be given once you play through the prologue. This item gives the soul the abilities you need to survive.

    The item must be your most prized possession from before you died. There is no hidden ability here, only the item and its description. Extra lore is allowed.
